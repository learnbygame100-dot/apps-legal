# Privacy Policy — Learn Imla

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy for Avner Avigad (doing business as **Learn Imla**) ("we", "us", or "our") describes how and why we collect, store, use, and share ("process") your personal information when you:

- Visit our website at [learnimla.com](https://learnimla.com)
- Download and use our mobile application, Learn Imla
- Use Learn Imla — an educational app for children and teens that teaches English grammar, vocabulary, math, and Hebrew spelling through practice, quizzes, and games. Parents, teachers, and schools create and manage children's accounts and follow their progress.

**Questions or concerns?** We are responsible for deciding how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use Learn Imla. Contact us at learnbygame100@gmail.com.

## 1. What information do we collect?

### Personal information you provide to us

We collect personal information that you provide when you register, when you add a child or a class, when you use the app, or when you contact us. Depending on your role (parent, Solo learner, teacher, or school administrator) this may include:

- names
- email addresses
- phone numbers
- usernames
- passwords, and for class students a PIN or one-time sign-in code (stored only in hashed form)
- contact preferences (notification settings, reminder time, marketing opt-in)
- a child's date of birth (used to suggest the right level) and gender (used to draw the avatar)
- school or class name, and class membership

**Sensitive information.** We process student data — learning records such as answers, scores, stars, homework, and progress — to provide the service. See [section 12](#12-childrens-privacy-and-parental-consent).

**Payment data.** Payments are made on the website only, through Paddle, which acts as Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and stores your card details; we never see or store them. We receive only the subscription status, plan, and billing dates. Children cannot make purchases. Paddle's privacy notice: [paddle.com/legal/privacy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).

**Phone number and SMS verification.** If you sign in with a phone number, we send a one-time verification code by SMS through Firebase Authentication (Google). We use your number only to verify you and sign you in, never for marketing, and it is deleted with your account. Standard message and data rates may apply.

### Information automatically collected

When you use Learn Imla we automatically collect:

- **Log and usage data** — which features and screens you use, dates and times, and error and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).
- **Device data** — device model, operating system, app version, browser type, IP address, and app and device identifiers.
- **Approximate location** — the country and city derived from your IP address by Google Analytics. We never request or collect precise GPS location.
- **Notification token** — if you allow notifications, a device token so we can deliver them (see [Notifications](#notifications)).

This information is used to keep the service secure and working, to fix problems, and for internal analytics. **We collect no analytics and no crash reports while a child is signed in.** From adult sessions, analytics use a randomly generated ID, never your name; you can switch analytics and crash reports off for your own account in Settings → "Help improve the app". Advertising ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are switched off in the app.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging; Apple Push Notification service on iOS), your time zone, and your chosen reminder time. We use them for practice reminders, service messages (for example "your child completed an exam" or "homework is due tomorrow"), and cheers or feedback from a parent or teacher. Marketing notifications (new content) are sent only if you opt in, and you can turn them off in Account Settings → Notifications. Scheduled notifications are sent only between 08:00 and 20:00 your local time; urgent billing notices may arrive outside those hours. Turning notifications off in your device settings stops them; the token is deleted with your account.

### Information collected from other sources

- **Teachers and schools.** When a teacher or school adds a child to a class, they give us the child's first name and the parent's email address. We then email the parent for consent (see [section 12](#12-childrens-privacy-and-parental-consent)).
- **Paddle.** After a payment, Paddle tells us the subscription status, plan, and billing dates.

We do not obtain information from data brokers, marketing partners, or social media, and we do not use any information for targeted advertising.

## 2. How do we process your information?

We process your personal information to:

- create and manage accounts and let you sign in

- report learning progress to the parents, teachers, and school administrators who manage a child

- protect someone's vital interests where necessary to prevent harm

We process your information for other purposes only with your prior consent.

## 3. What legal bases do we rely on?

**If you are in the EU or UK,** we rely on:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the service you or your child's parent or school signed up for.
- **Consent** — for a child's account (given by the parent), marketing notifications, and analytics where required. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legitimate interests** — to analyse how the service is used so we can improve it, and to diagnose problems and prevent fraud, where these interests are not outweighed by your rights.
- **Legal obligations** — to comply with the law, cooperate with authorities, or defend our legal rights.
- **Vital interests** — where necessary to protect someone's safety.

**If you are in Canada,** we process your information with your express or implied consent, which you can withdraw at any time, or where the law permits processing without consent (for example, fraud prevention or compliance with a court order).

## 4. When and with whom do we share your personal information?

We share personal information only with service providers that run Learn Imla on our behalf, under contracts that allow them to use it only on our instructions:

| Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|
| Database, file storage, hosting, sign-in and SMS codes, push notifications | Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Hosting, Authentication, Cloud Messaging) on Google Cloud Platform |

- **Legal requirements.** When required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## 7. How long do we keep your information?

We keep your personal information while your account exists, and delete it within one month after the account is deleted — unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, Paddle keeps invoice records for tax purposes).

- If a subscription ends and is not renewed, we keep the account for 60 days — so a lapsed payment or a break between terms does not lose your children's progress, and you have time to export it — and then delete it.
- If a pupil added by a school leaves their last class, their data is kept for 30 days so a parent can download or delete it, and is then deleted.
- If a parent does not confirm a class invitation, the consent link expires after 14 days, and the details the school entered are deleted about two weeks after that.
- Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months and is not linked to your name.

## 8. How do we keep your information safe?

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules that limit each user to their own data, Firebase App Check to block unauthorised apps, and hashed storage of PINs and sign-in codes. No system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

## 12. Children's privacy and parental consent

Learn Imla is designed for children. Children cannot create an account on their own: a child's profile is created either by a parent from the parent's own account, or by a teacher or school for a class. When a teacher or school adds a child, we email the parent and the child's account is not activated until the parent confirms by clicking the link in that email. If the parent does not confirm, the link expires after 14 days and the details the school entered are deleted about two weeks after that. Learners aged 13 or older may register a Solo account themselves.

From children we collect only what the app needs to work: first name, username, date of birth, gender, sign-in details (a PIN or one-time code), and learning activity such as answers, scores, and progress. A child profile has no email address or phone number of its own, and no way to message anyone outside the child's own class. We do not collect a child's location, contacts, photos, or audio. We do not show ads to children, we do not sell or share their data for advertising, and children cannot make purchases.

A parent can at any time review the information we hold about their child, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent, by emailing learnbygame100@gmail.com or by using "Manage my data" in the app. We delete the child's data within 30 days of the request.

If you believe a child has used Learn Imla without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete the information.
